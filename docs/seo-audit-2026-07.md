## SERP picture

cobl.ai appears in none of the 7 SERPs checked. Every query triggered a Google AI Overview, so citability matters as much as blue links.

- "AI proposal generator": won by design/doc tools (Venngage, Proposify, Better Proposals), all leading with Free. Softest entry.
- "AI RFP software": the AI-native wave (Inventive AI, DeepRFP, Contravault, Iris AI) plus Gartner, Loopio, Responsive. That wave is Cobl's direct competitive set.
- "RFP response software": incumbent territory (Gartner, Responsive, Loopio).
- "autonomous RFP generation": weak SERP (a dev.to post ranks #1), trivially winnable if the term takes off.
- "logiciel appel d'offres": tender-sourcing portals, no AI-response player. Gap for Cobl.
- "memoire technique appel d'offre": all guides, no strong SaaS. Very winnable with template plus guide content.
- "expert IA Nantes": agency-shaped SERP (Sortlist, Sociaty, Drakkar, Kayro). axelrock.fr absent; a dedicated page could crack the top 10.

## Backlink verdict

axelrock.com: 32 backlinks, 31 referring domains, all to the homepage, mostly junk except authority.dejan.ai. axelrock.fr: 44 backlinks, 32 domains, mostly homepage, one legit dofollow from lense.fr. A blanket 301 of axelrock.com to axelrock.fr is fully justified: zero deep-link equity to preserve.

## On-page (axelrock.fr)

Score 98.9/100, fast and clean, but thin: low word count caps rankings, zero internal links on the homepage (nothing points to the blog), title is 32 chars and meta description 83 chars, both shorter than ideal. The description should work in the Nantes/IA keywords.

## Spend

$0.43 total for the audit. Balance $50.57 remaining of $51.

## Implications

1. For Cobl, the memoire technique template/guide cluster is the highest-leverage FR content play, and "AI RFP software" the EN momentum target.
2. "autonomous RFP generation" is a category-creation positioning term to seed in prose, LinkedIn, and video, not a keyword page.
3. For axelrock.fr: add homepage internal links to the blog, lengthen the title and meta description with Nantes/IA terms, and grow homepage content.
4. Blanket 301s are safe.
